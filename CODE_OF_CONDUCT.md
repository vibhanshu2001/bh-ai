# Community guidelines

bh-ai welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. These
guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Help newcomers and respect differing perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy. Obtain permission before sharing another person's information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, and spam are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private
contact channel. If none is listed, request a private channel in a public issue
without naming affected people or posting sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict
participation based on the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Reports
will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved. Contributors are
expected to cooperate with moderation requests.
